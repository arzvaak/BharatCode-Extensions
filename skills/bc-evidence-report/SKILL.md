---
name: bc-evidence-report
description: "Collect sources and write an evidence-backed research report when requested."
---

Record the question and scope. Collect sources through available research tools and keep source URLs with each claim. Separate observed facts from inference. Inspect conflicting evidence. Draft a report with citations and limitations, save the resulting artifact, and give the user its location. Never invent a source or claim a document was inspected when only its metadata was available.
